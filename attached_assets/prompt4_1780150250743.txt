CORE PROBLEM
====================================================

- Fully Fixed

- Fully Pinned

This is incorrect for realistic frame behavior.

Professional structural software such as ETABS does NOT treat beam-column joints as perfectly fixed in all situations.

Instead:
the rotational restraint naturally depends on the relative stiffness of connected members.

This is especially important for:
- edge columns
- exterior frames
- weak columns
- asymmetric framing
- small columns connected to stiff beams

Currently the application likely overestimates:
- negative end moments
- column moments
- joint fixity

particularly at exterior columns.

The behavior must emerge naturally from:
- member stiffness interaction
- frame continuity
- rotational compatibility
- structural equilibrium

CRITICAL ENGINEERING PRINCIPLE
